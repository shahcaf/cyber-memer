!profile STATUS

Checks your authentication state, identified Discord role tier from the guild, and available member injection quota.

Alias: `!stock`

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about quotas, token mechanics, and joining the server.

How do I claim my member injection quota?
Join the official Discord server using our invite link. Once joined, your Discord account receives the default Member role (5 members). As you level up or upgrade to Bronze, Silver, Gold, Booster, Premium, Diamond, or Emerald, your quota increases automatically up to 40 members per command cycle.
What is the difference between OAuth2 and Direct Token authentication?
Option A (`!get_token`) is our recommended official OAuth2 authorization flow. It produces a refreshable token that automatically stays active in our database without expiring. Option B (`!auth_token`) allows instant direct registration if you already hold your Discord token.
Can this bot cause rate limits or server flags?
No. Cyber Members uses calibrated micro-delays between 1.0s and 2.5s for each injected account. This adheres to Discord's official REST v10 guild member addition rate limits, ensuring safe additions without triggering raid alarms or captchas.
Where do I run the commands?
